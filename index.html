Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MEM PLANETE ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our applications and services, including our use of the Amazon Advertising API.

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize our applications to access your Amazon account through Login with Amazon (LWA), we only request the minimum permissions necessary to provide advertising management and analytics services. Specifically, we request the advertising::campaign_management scope, which grants access to your Amazon Advertising account data such as campaign settings, ad group settings, keyword performance, and search term reports.

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, shipping address, payment information, or browsing history outside of the Amazon Advertising API scope.

2. How We Use Information

Data accessed via the Amazon Advertising API is used solely to:

  • Display advertising performance metrics to the account owner.
  • Generate optimization recommendations for advertising campaigns.
  • Execute approved changes to advertising settings on the account owner's behalf.

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer any data obtained through the Amazon Advertising API to any third party for advertising, marketing, or any other commercial purpose.

3. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and refresh tokens obtained from Login with Amazon are stored securely and used solely to authenticate API requests to the Amazon Advertising API on behalf of the authorized user. We do not store advertising performance data persistently; data is processed in-memory and presented to the user in real time.

4. Third-Party Services

Our applications interact only with official Amazon APIs (Amazon Advertising API). We do not integrate with any other third-party service that processes your Amazon account data.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You may revoke our application's access to your Amazon account at any time by visiting the Amazon "Login with Amazon" page under your account settings and removing the application. Upon revocation, we will delete any locally stored credentials associated with your account.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through the Amazon seller-to-developer communication channel authorized for our application.
